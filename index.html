Firmament

Clients anywhere in the world book and pay for a consultation with a Montreal consultant, without anyone doing time-zone maths.

  • Time-zone aware booking. Slots show in the visitor's own time zone; the consultant's calendar stays in Montreal time.
  • Two currencies. Stripe checkout in CAD or MAD, priced by session length.
  • Admin dashboard to block days or hours, manage appointments and review uploaded documents.
  • Reminder emails in English and French, sent automatically before each appointment.
  • No double bookings, even when two people pick the same slot at the same moment.

Deleveryno

A cash-on-delivery platform that connects online sellers, delivery drivers and an operations team in one place.

  • Three roles, one app: sellers create orders, admins assign drivers, drivers update deliveries from their phone.
  • The real delivery lifecycle: pending, assigned, in transit, delivered, plus no answer, postponed and cancelled.
  • Seller stock with admin approval, and built-in messaging for the team.
  • Made for drivers' phones: one tap to call the customer or open the address in Google Maps.
  • Arabic, French and English interface, with right-to-left layout for Arabic.

Trust Cairn

Immigration consultants must reconcile the money they hold for clients every month. Trust Cairn does it from their bank's own emails.

  • Reads bank notifications (Interac e-Transfers, deposits) forwarded from Outlook or Gmail, and turns them into entries waiting for approval.
  • Per-client balances across several bank accounts and currencies, with advances tracked until repaid.
  • Monthly reconciliation reports in PDF, Excel and CSV, ready for a regulator's inspection.
  • Full audit trail: every change records who made it, with before and after values.
  • Multi-tenant, background jobs, 165 automated tests.
